See Exactly What Happened to Your Drives

Most companies take your drives and that’s the last you hear. We show you everything. Log into your portal, enter a serial number, and see the test report for that drive. No guessing, no waiting on an email.

Money Sitting in Your Drawer

Old drives lose value the longer they sit. If you have hard drives from upgrades, retired servers, or a clean-out, they may be worth more than you think. Ship them to us and turn unused hardware into cash.

What About Drives That Fail?

Not every drive passes. If one fails testing, your batch report lists it and we ask what you’d like us to do with it. You decide, and nothing gets lost.

R2v3 Certified. Your Data Is in Responsible Hands.

Handing over hard drives means handing over trust. That’s why we’re R2v3 certified, the industry standard for responsible electronics reuse, recycling, and data security. It means our processes for handling, erasing, and tracking your drives have been independently audited, not just promised.
